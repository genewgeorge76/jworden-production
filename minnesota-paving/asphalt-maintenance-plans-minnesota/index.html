The Economics: Why Planned Maintenance Wins

Pavement follows a well-documented decline curve. For roughly the first three-quarters of its life, asphalt loses condition slowly — then it drops off a cliff, and the cost to restore it multiplies. The widely cited pavement-preservation finding is that a dollar spent on timely maintenance in that early window saves six to ten dollars of reconstruction later. A maintenance plan exists to keep your surface on the cheap part of that curve instead of letting it fall off the edge.

In Minnesota the stakes are higher because the enemy — water and freeze-thaw — never takes a year off. Miss a crack-filling season and the next winter drives water straight into the base. Planned maintenance removes that risk by scheduling the right work at the right time, automatically.

What a Plan Covers

  • Scheduled inspections that catch distress while it is still cheap to fix.
  • Crack filling on a recurring basis so water never reaches the base.
  • Sealcoating at the correct 2-to-3-year interval for your traffic level.
  • Prioritized repairs with clear budget forecasting so there are no surprises.
  • Seasonal timing built around Minnesota's May-through-September work window.

Who This Is For

  • Property managers overseeing multiple parking lots
  • Businesses prioritizing appearance and safety

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an asphalt maintenance plan include?

Scheduled inspections, crack monitoring and filling, sealcoating at the right interval, and prioritized repair recommendations with budget forecasting — scaling from one driveway to multiple commercial lots.

Does preventive maintenance actually save money?

Yes — timely maintenance saves several dollars in future reconstruction for every dollar spent, by keeping water out of the base.

How often should asphalt be sealcoated and crack-filled?

Sealcoat every 2 to 3 years and crack-fill annually or as new cracks appear. A plan schedules these automatically.

Do you offer plans for seasonal lake properties?

Yes — we coordinate around seasonal occupancy for lake cabins and summer homes so the driveway is maintained even when you are away.
