Sealcoating FAQ — Pine River, MN

What does sealcoating cost in Pine River?

Residential driveways typically run $300–$600. Commercial lots are quoted by square footage — call for a free on-site estimate.

When can I sealcoat in Pine River?

The Pine River sealcoating season runs May through September. Surface temperature must be 50°F+ with no rain in the 24-hour forecast.

How long does sealcoat last in Cass County?

With proper application, sealcoating lasts 2–3 years in the Pine River area before the next application is needed. Deep seasonal frost means regular maintenance is critical.

How We Sealcoat in Pine River

Every Pine River sealcoating job follows the same prep-first process — the steps that decide whether a coat lasts one season or several.

1. Clean & Prep

We power-clean the surface, clear vegetation from cracks, and treat oil spots so the sealer bonds. Skipping prep is the number-one cause of early failure.

2. Crack Fill & Repair

Cracks over 1/4" get hot-pour rubberized filler (ASTM D6690); potholes and failed spots are patched before any coating goes down.

3. Two-Coat Application

Polymer-modified emulsion with silica sand for traction, applied in two squeegee coats for full, even coverage built for Cass County freeze-thaw.

4. Cure & Inspect

We keep traffic off 24–48 hours, then inspect. Commercial lots get re-striping, and you get a simple re-seal schedule.
