Driveway Sealcoating Prep Checklist for Minnesota Homeowners

Published 2026-05-29 · By Jordan Worden · Minnesota Asphalt Paving

Before Your Appointment

  • Clear all vehicles from the driveway and nearby turnaround area
  • Trim low branches and remove obstacles from pavement edges
  • Stop sprinkler schedules at least 24 hours before service
  • Confirm pets and children can be kept off the driveway during cure time

Surface Condition Checklist

  • Mark oil spots so your contractor can treat them first
  • Identify wide cracks and failed areas needing repair before coating
  • Note low spots where water ponds after rainfall
  • Verify downspouts are not dumping directly onto asphalt

Weather and Timing Requirements

In Minnesota, sealcoating quality depends heavily on weather. Surface and ambient conditions should support proper curing, and rain risk should be low throughout the planned cure window.

After Application

  • Follow your contractor's cure-time guidance before driving
  • Keep sharp kickstands and heavy trailer jacks off fresh coating
  • Avoid power washing immediately after curing
  • Plan crack inspections annually to protect your coating investment
