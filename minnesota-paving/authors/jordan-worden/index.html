Author Profile

Jordan Worden

4th-generation paving operator publishing Minnesota-specific asphalt guidance based on field execution, not generic theory.

Professional Background

Jordan Worden leads content development for Minnesota Asphalt Paving and contributes operational standards used by crews serving Brainerd, Walker, Bemidji, St. Cloud, and surrounding communities.

His focus is translating real-world paving and maintenance decisions into practical homeowner and property manager guidance that improves project outcomes and extends pavement life.

Topics Covered

  • Driveway thickness and base construction for frost-prone conditions
  • Timing and preparation for sealcoating in Minnesota weather windows
  • Repair strategy for spring potholes, cracking, and edge deterioration
  • Maintenance planning for residential and commercial asphalt surfaces

Editorial Standards

Every guide is reviewed for climate relevance, code/practice accuracy, and applicability to Minnesota service conditions.
