Our Proven Paving Process

Premium results come from doing the unglamorous steps right. Here is exactly how we build a driveway in Motley that lasts.

1. Site Evaluation

We measure on site, check drainage and soil, and tell you honestly whether you need new pavement, an overlay, or just maintenance.

2. Base & Grading

The base is everything in frost country. We excavate, install and compact a proper Class 5 aggregate base, and build in crown and slope so water sheds away.

3. Paving & Compaction

Hot-mix asphalt with PG 58-34 binder, laid at the correct thickness and rolled to full density for a tight, durable mat.

4. Seal & Maintain

After a proper cure, we sealcoat and set you up on a simple crack-fill and re-seal cycle that can double the life of the surface.

Why Motley Property Owners Choose Us

Motley's long rural driveways and riverfront properties see less traffic but far more weather exposure, which makes correct construction and regular sealcoating essential to preventing oxidation and freeze-thaw damage.

We use polymer-modified asphalt emulsion sealcoating — never coal tar, which is banned in Minneapolis and St. Paul and increasingly restricted statewide. Our products are formulated for Minnesota's extreme temperature swings, flexing with freeze-thaw cycles instead of cracking. Every job includes surface prep, crack filling, two-coat squeegee application, and a 24–48 hour cure inspection.

Motley Paving & Sealcoating FAQ

How much does asphalt paving cost in Motley?

Residential driveway paving in Motley typically runs $3–$6 per square foot depending on size, base preparation, drainage, and site access. Long rural driveways are priced per project after an on-site measurement. We provide free, no-obligation estimates.

When is the best time to pave or sealcoat in Motley?

The Motley paving season runs May through September. New asphalt should cure 30–90 days before its first sealcoat, and sealcoating needs surface temperatures above 50°F with no rain in the 24-hour forecast. Book by July for best availability.

Do you pave long rural and lake-country driveways?

Yes — long private driveways and cabin access roads along the Crow Wing River and Highway 10 corridor are a core part of our work. We engineer proper base depth, crown, and drainage for remote rural sites.

Why is sealcoating important in Morrison County's climate?

Motley sees deep frost and dozens of freeze-thaw cycles each year. Sealcoating locks out the water that drives cracking and raveling, and our polymer-modified emulsion flexes with temperature swings instead of cracking like coal tar.

Do you offer commercial parking lot work in Motley?

Yes. We pave, overlay, sealcoat, crack-fill, and re-stripe parking lots for businesses, churches, and municipal properties throughout Morrison County, with ADA-compliant layouts.

What areas around Motley do you serve?

We serve Motley and surrounding communities including Staples, Pillager, Little Falls, Randall, and Lincoln, plus the wider Brainerd Lakes and central Minnesota region.
