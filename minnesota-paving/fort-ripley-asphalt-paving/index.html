Why Fort Ripley Needs a Northern Minnesota Specialist

Fort Ripley's military and residential properties both need regular sealcoating. Heavy military vehicle traffic on access roads and parking areas accelerates surface wear.

We use polymer-modified asphalt emulsion sealcoating — not coal tar (banned in Minneapolis/St. Paul and increasingly restricted statewide). Our products are formulated for Minnesota's extreme temperature swings, flexing with freeze-thaw cycles instead of cracking. Every job includes surface prep, crack filling, two-coat squeegee application, and a 24–48 hour cure inspection.

How We Pave in Fort Ripley

Every Fort Ripley driveway and lot follows the same base-first process — the steps that decide whether asphalt lasts 8 years or 20+ in Crow Wing County.

1. Site Evaluation

We measure on site, check drainage and soil, and tell you honestly whether you need new pavement, an overlay, or maintenance.

2. Base & Grading

The base is everything in frost country. We excavate, install and compact a proper Class 5 aggregate base, and build in crown and slope so water sheds away.

3. Paving & Compaction

Hot-mix asphalt with PG 58-34 binder, laid at the correct thickness and rolled to full density for a tight, durable mat.

4. Seal & Maintain

After curing, we sealcoat and set you up on a simple crack-fill and re-seal cycle that can double the life of the surface.
