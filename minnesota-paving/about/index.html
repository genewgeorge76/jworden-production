Built Around Minnesota, Not a Brochure

Most paving advice is written for mild climates. Minnesota is not mild. Our winters drive water deep into the ground, freeze it, and heave everything above it; our springs turn subgrade to mush; our summers bake the surface. A driveway or lot that survives here has to be built for that cycle from the base up. Everything we do starts from that reality — we'd rather fix the drainage and the base correctly than sell a pretty surface that fails in three winters.

What We Stand For

  • Climate-specific asphalt strategy, not one-size-fits-all methods
  • Drainage and structure before cosmetic treatments
  • Clear scope recommendations and transparent communication
  • Maintenance planning that protects long-term value
  • Honest assessments — if an overlay will do, we won't sell you a teardown

Primary Client Segments

  • Rural homeowners with long and load-bearing driveways
  • Lake-cabin and summer-home owners requiring seasonal planning
  • Commercial and mixed-use properties requiring uptime and safety

Where We Work
