Why St. Joseph Needs a Northern Minnesota Specialist

St. Joseph's university parking lots, student housing complexes, and established residential neighborhoods all need professional sealcoating services on regular maintenance cycles.

We use polymer-modified asphalt emulsion sealcoating — not coal tar (banned in Minneapolis/St. Paul and increasingly restricted statewide). Our products are formulated for Minnesota's extreme temperature swings, flexing with freeze-thaw cycles instead of cracking. Every job includes surface prep, crack filling, two-coat squeegee application, and a 24–48 hour cure inspection.

How much does asphalt paving cost in St. Joseph?

Residential driveway paving in St. Joseph typically runs $3–$6 per square foot depending on size, base preparation needs, and site accessibility. We provide free on-site estimates with no obligation.

When is the best time to pave or sealcoat in St. Joseph?

The St. Joseph paving season runs May through September. Sealcoating requires surface temperatures above 50°F with no rain in the 24-hour forecast. We recommend scheduling by July for best availability.

How We Pave in St. Joseph

Every St. Joseph driveway and lot follows the same base-first process — the steps that decide whether asphalt lasts 8 years or 20+ in Stearns County.

1. Site Evaluation

We measure on site, check drainage and soil, and tell you honestly whether you need new pavement, an overlay, or maintenance.

2. Base & Grading

The base is everything in frost country. We excavate, install and compact a proper Class 5 aggregate base, and build in crown and slope so water sheds away.

3. Paving & Compaction

Hot-mix asphalt with PG 58-34 binder, laid at the correct thickness and rolled to full density for a tight, durable mat.

4. Seal & Maintain

After curing, we sealcoat and set you up on a simple crack-fill and re-seal cycle that can double the life of the surface.
