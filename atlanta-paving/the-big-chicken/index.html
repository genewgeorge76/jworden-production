Why a QSR lot is its own problem

A drive-thru lane is not a parking lot. Traffic stops, idles and creeps in the same wheel path every day, and heat does the rest — an under-specified binder ruts and shoves at the order board and the window long before anything cracks elsewhere. Georgia summers make that the failure mode to design against, which is why binder grade and compaction carry a restaurant lot rather than mat thickness alone.

Standard

GDOT specifications over a base built to drain, with a 96% Marshall Unit Weight minimum compaction floor.
